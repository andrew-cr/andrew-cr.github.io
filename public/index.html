Bio

I am a fourth year PhD student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford. My supervisors are Arnaud Doucet and Tom Rainforth.

During my PhD, I have worked on expanding the denoising diffusion methodology to a wider range of data types including discrete data and transdimensional data. I have applied these methods to scientific data including molecules and proteins.

Before coming to Oxford, I completed a Masters in Information Engineering at the University of Cambridge where I was supervised by José Miguel Hernández-Lobato.
